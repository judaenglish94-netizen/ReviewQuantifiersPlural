Reference Reading: A Busy Saturday in the Valley

Scrollable Text

On sunny autumn mornings, many people leave the busy city center to enjoy quiet moments in the mountain valley. Groups of young men and women walk together along the rustic wooden bridges, carrying heavy backpacks filled with fresh apples and cold drinks. Families bring their energetic children, who run through the tall green grass and collect colorful falling leaves under the ancient trees. Near the cold rivers, small gray mice hide inside hollow logs while wild wolves watch quietly from high up on the rocky hills.

Local farmers work hard every day near their large barns to protect their quiet farm animals. A farmer checks his white sheep standing near the wooden fences and feeds the noisy geese splashing in the muddy ponds. His tired feet hurt after working many hours on the farm, but he always takes good care of his animals. He carefully sharpens heavy metal knives to cut fresh bread and vegetables, preparing a warm meal for his big family after a long day.

In the clean blue water of the river, silver fish swim quickly past bright green plants and smooth stones. Young tourists love taking beautiful photos of the natural scenery while keeping their cameras completely steady with both hands. Some visitors sit quietly on wide stone benches, smiling and showing their white teeth while talking about their pleasant lives in the countryside. As the sun sets behind the dark mountains, all the travelers head back to their cozy cabins, happy to spend another wonderful weekend surrounded by peaceful nature.
